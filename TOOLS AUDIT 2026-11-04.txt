Tests: yooper-trivia\tests\test_audit.js, gift-finder\tests\test_audit.js, and a new weak signal check in

YOOPER TRIVIA: WHAT WAS WRONG AND WHAT WAS FIXED
1. Soo Locks year had two right answers. The question asked for "the first Soo Locks" and offered 1798, but the
   same source page says the first lock on the St. Marys River was built on the Canadian side in 1798.
   Fixed: the question now asks for the first U.S. Soo Locks, and 1798 is replaced with 1825.
2. Pine Mountain was called the largest ski jump in the United States. The source says "Excluding ski flying hills".
   Copper Peak near Ironwood is bigger. Fixed: the question and quote keep "not counting ski flying hills".
3. "Almost half of the Finnish immigrants to the U.S. settled in the U.P." The Wikipedia sentence has no citation, and
   Wikipedia's own Finnish Americans page counts more Finnish Americans in Minnesota than in all of Michigan.
   Fixed: replaced with a cited fact. The western U.P. has the only U.S. counties where Finnish is the biggest ancestry.
4. The Ironwood question says 52-foot, but the quote shown to players did not include the height.
   Fixed: the quote now carries "a 52 ft (15.8 m) fiberglass statue".
5. The pasty question said "in the 1800s", which the quote did not show. Fixed: "19th century", with that part quoted.
6. "About how long is the Mackinac Bridge?" with the answer "4.995 miles" read oddly. Fixed: "Just under 5 miles (4.995)".
7. A wrong answer showed "The answer is About 48 feet." with a capital letter mid-sentence.
   Fixed: "The right answer: About 48 feet".
8. Weak signal: the page waited for the network with no time limit, so one bar at camp meant a blank page.
   Fixed: after 3 seconds the saved copy is used, the same way the gift finder already worked.
   Shown failing on the old file (PASS=14 FAIL=1), passing on the new one (PASS=15 FAIL=0).
New general rule in the test: every number in a question must appear in its quote.
